================================================================
FILE 12: GOVERNMENT ECONOMIC SCHEMES EVERY INDIAN SHOULD KNOW
================================================================
Schemes that directly benefit ordinary citizens — eligibility, benefits, how to apply
================================================================

WHY GOVERNMENT SCHEMES MATTER MORE THAN MOST PEOPLE REALISE
================================================================
Millions of Indian citizens are unaware of government schemes they are fully eligible 
for and simply not using. This is a form of real financial loss — leaving money and 
protection on the table. The schemes covered here are either universally applicable 
to working Indians or targeted at specific income groups that represent the majority 
of the country.

================================================================
SCHEME 1: PRADHAN MANTRI JAN DHAN YOJANA (PMJDY)
================================================================
What it is: A basic bank account with no minimum balance requirement, built-in 
accidental insurance cover, and a RuPay debit card with ₹2 lakh accidental death 
insurance benefit. The account also provides access to the overdraft facility 
of up to ₹10,000 after 6 months of active usage.

Who needs it: Anyone without a bank account, particularly daily wage workers, domestic 
workers, agricultural labourers, and people in rural areas.

Why it matters economically: A bank account is the foundation of all financial 
participation. Without one, you cannot receive government benefits, subsidies, or 
formal salary payments. The PMJDY has already connected over 500 million Indians 
to the banking system.

How to apply: Visit any nationalised or private bank with your Aadhaar card and 
a passport-size photograph. No income proof required. Account opened same day.

================================================================
SCHEME 2: PRADHAN MANTRI SURAKSHA BIMA YOJANA (PMSBY)
================================================================
What it is: Accidental death and disability insurance of ₹2 lakh for a premium 
of only ₹20 per year (less than ₹2 per month). This is possibly the best-value 
insurance product in existence globally for a low-income household.

Eligibility: Any Indian citizen aged 18-70 with a bank account.

Coverage: ₹2 lakh for accidental death or total permanent disability, ₹1 lakh for 
partial permanent disability.

The staggering value: For a family earning ₹15,000/month, the ₹20 annual premium 
is 0.01% of annual income. The ₹2 lakh payout in case of accidental death is more 
than a year's income — providing critical support to dependents. Every person with 
dependents should have this without exception.

How to apply: Any bank branch or online banking portal. Automatically deducted 
from your bank account annually every June 1st.

================================================================
SCHEME 3: PRADHAN MANTRI JEEVAN JYOTI BIMA YOJANA (PMJJBY)
================================================================
What it is: Life insurance (death from any cause — not just accident) of ₹2 lakh 
for a premium of ₹436 per year (approximately ₹36 per month).

Eligibility: Age 18-50 with a bank account.

Difference from PMSBY: PMSBY only covers accidental death. PMJJBY covers all causes 
of death — including illness, natural causes, etc. Together, both schemes cost only 
₹456/year and provide ₹4 lakh combined coverage. For a household without any 
private life insurance, this combination is a minimum baseline.

Limitation: The ₹2 lakh coverage is modest — for the primary income earner of a 
family, supplementing with a private term insurance plan providing ₹50 lakh to 
₹1 crore is strongly advisable if income allows.

================================================================
SCHEME 4: ATAL PENSION YOJANA (APY)
================================================================
What it is: A government-backed pension scheme for workers in the unorganised 
sector — those without any Provident Fund or pension from their employer.

How it works: You contribute a fixed monthly amount based on your age and desired 
pension. At age 60, you receive a guaranteed monthly pension of ₹1,000 to ₹5,000 
(you choose the amount at enrollment). The government also co-contributes for 
eligible low-income participants.

Target audience: Domestic workers, daily wage laborers, gig economy workers, 
self-employed individuals, small traders — anyone without a corporate PF.

Key numbers: A 25-year-old enrolling for ₹5,000/month pension at age 60 pays 
only ₹376/month in contributions. A 35-year-old pays ₹902/month for the same 
₹5,000/month pension. Starting early makes a dramatic difference in affordability.

After the subscriber's death, the spouse receives the same monthly pension. After 
both die, the accumulated corpus (approximately ₹8.5 lakh for the ₹5,000/month 
plan) is returned to the nominee.

================================================================
SCHEME 5: PM KISAN SAMMAN NIDHI
================================================================
What it is: Direct income support of ₹6,000 per year (₹2,000 every 4 months) 
transferred directly to the bank accounts of eligible farmers.

Eligibility: Small and marginal farmers owning cultivable land — family income 
does not determine eligibility, land ownership does.

Why it matters: For millions of farm families, this ₹6,000 annual transfer, while 
modest in absolute terms, arrives at predictable intervals and provides a minimum 
income floor. Combined with PMSBY and PMJJBY, it forms a basic social safety net.

================================================================
SCHEME 6: AYUSHMAN BHARAT — PRADHAN MANTRI JAN AROGYA YOJANA (PM-JAY)
================================================================
What it is: Health insurance of ₹5 lakh per family per year for hospitalisation 
costs, covering treatment in empanelled government and private hospitals. It is 
free for eligible families — there is no premium to pay.

Eligibility: Families listed in the SECC 2011 (Socio-Economic Caste Census) database, 
broadly covering economically vulnerable households. Several states have expanded 
coverage to a wider population — check your state government's version.

What is covered: Over 1,500 treatment packages including surgeries, chemotherapy, 
dialysis, mental health care, and neonatal care. Cashless treatment available at 
over 27,000 empanelled hospitals nationwide.

Why this is transformative: Before PM-JAY, a hospitalisation requiring major surgery 
could push a lower-income family into permanent debt. This scheme eliminates that 
catastrophic financial risk for eligible families.

================================================================
SCHEME 7: NATIONAL PENSION SYSTEM (NPS) — GOVERNMENT CONTRIBUTION FOR CENTRAL GOVT EMPLOYEES
================================================================
For government employees, NPS includes a government matching contribution of 14% of 
basic salary + DA. For private sector employees, there is no government matching 
contribution — but the tax benefits (Section 80C + additional Section 80CCD(1B) of 
₹50,000) make NPS attractive as a voluntary retirement savings tool.

================================================================
SCHEME 8: PRADHAN MANTRI MUDRA YOJANA (PMMY) — FOR SMALL BUSINESS OWNERS
================================================================
What it is: Collateral-free loans for small and micro businesses in three categories:
  Shishu: Up to ₹50,000 (for businesses just starting)
  Kishor: ₹50,001 to ₹5 lakh (for established micro businesses)
  Tarun: ₹5 lakh to ₹10 lakh (for small businesses looking to expand)

No collateral or security is required. Available through all banks, MFIs, NBFCs, 
and Small Finance Banks. Aimed at self-employed individuals, small manufacturers, 
shopkeepers, service providers, and artisans.

The interest rate is not regulated by the scheme — banks set their own rates. 
Typically, Mudra loans are available at 8.5% to 12% per annum. This is significantly 
cheaper than informal moneylender rates (which can be 24-60%) but more expensive 
than formal large business loans (6-8%). For genuinely viable small businesses 
without access to formal credit, Mudra is a legitimate pathway to affordable capital.

================================================================
HOW TO CHECK ALL SCHEMES YOU ARE ELIGIBLE FOR
================================================================
The government runs the MyScheme portal at myscheme.gov.in where you can enter 
your details (age, gender, income, occupation, state) and receive a personalised 
list of schemes you are eligible for across Central and State governments. This is 
the most efficient way to ensure you are not leaving any government benefit unused.

For Aadhaar-linked subsidy verification, visit uidai.gov.in to check which subsidies 
are currently being credited to your Aadhaar-linked bank accounts.
